ABOUT ST. ROSE

Education with Purpose.

St. Rose College Educational Foundation, Inc. is an educational institution serving students in Paniqui, Tarlac and surrounding communities.

The institution aims to provide students with opportunities to develop academically, professionally, and personally while preparing them for meaningful careers and responsible citizenship.
